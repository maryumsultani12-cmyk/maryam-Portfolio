Case Study — 03

QuickSnacks — Recipe Website.

An informative recipe website featuring snack categories, recipe details, reviews, a gallery and location-based contact functionality.

HTMLCSSBootstrapJavaScriptJQuery
Overview

Why it exists.

A recipe site people can actually browse, not just read.

QuickSnacks is an informative recipe website built around snack categories, so visitors can jump straight to the kind of snack they're craving instead of scrolling through everything.

Each recipe page includes full details and reviews, backed by a gallery for visual browsing, plus location-based contact functionality for reaching out directly.

Snack category browsing
Recipe detail pages
Reviews on recipes
Photo gallery
Location-based contact
Build

How it was built.

Interactive, browser-side features layered on a simple structure.

Frontend. HTML, CSS and Bootstrap handle the responsive layout, while JavaScript and jQuery power the gallery interactions and the recipe review widgets.

Content structure. Snacks are grouped into categories first, so the site feels organized even as more recipes get added over time.

Contact. A location-aware contact section lets visitors reach out with context, rather than a generic form disconnected from where they are.

Outcome. A recipe site that's easy to browse visually and gives visitors a reason to come back — reviews and a gallery, not just static recipe text.
